# Lumi Beta Works Developer Rules

## Brand Philosophy
Lumi Beta Works started from a personal freelancer aiming to build a larger tech business.
* **Lumi**: The light of an idea.
* **Beta**: The courage to experiment.
* **Works**: Turning ideas into something real.

We serve enterprise clients but **we never forget MSMEs (UMKM)**.

## STRICT Copywriting Rules
1. **NO AI TONE**: Content must sound human, clean, and direct.
2. **NO "--"**: Do not use the double dash ("--") as a conversational filler or transition.
3. **NO FLUFF**: Avoid unnecessary "basa-basi". Be concise and to the point.
