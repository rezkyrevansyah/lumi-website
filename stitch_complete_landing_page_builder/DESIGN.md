## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
The design system establishes a high-credibility digital agency and technology studio presence. It evokes technical rigor, architectural clarity, and calm authority without appearing sterile or detached. Through a measured balance of deep charcoal typography, expansive pure white surfaces, and precision emerald accents, the interface signals production-grade engineering and human-centered design execution.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Archetype
The system operates at the intersection of **Contemporary Architectural Minimalism** and **Refined Glassmorphic Depth**:
- **Pure White & Subtle Alternation:** Expansive `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with `#FAFAFA` alternating bands create natural cadence across landing views without cluttering divider lines.
- **Diffuse Multi-Layer Soft Shadows:** Surfaces float naturally with wide, low-opacity ambient shadows instead of sharp lines or heavy skeuomorphic extrusions.
- **High-Character Display Typography:** `Bricolage Grotesque` lends an idiosyncratic, modern technical signature to editorial headlines, contrasted by the structural clarity of `Plus Jakarta Sans` across interface layers and body paragraphs.
- **Atmospheric Glow Overlays:** Subtle, ambient radial gradients (8% to 14% opacity) blending violet, cobalt blue, and emerald provide depth behind key architectural anchors without competing with readability.

## Colors

### Functional Color Allocation (One Color, One Meaning)
Every color role adheres to strict visual heuristics:
- **`#18181B` (Zinc-900 / Primary CTA & Display Headings):** Universally signals dominant hierarchy and primary actionability. All high-intent primary buttons, hero text, and section headers employ this dark tone, ensuring AAA contrast (8.9:1) against white.
- **`#047857` & `#065F46` (Emerald-700 & Emerald-800 / Secondary Action & Interactive Text):** Used exclusively for secondary interactive triggers and inline hyperlinks to maintain distinct interactive recognition (5.48:1 and 7.69:1 contrast).
- **`#10B981` (Emerald-500 / Identity & Accents):** Dedicated strictly to non-text glyphs, status dots, metric badges, and illustrative accents. Never applied to small body text.
- **`#ECFDF5` (Emerald-50 / Badge Fill):** Serves as the soft, tinted container fill for pills, tier tags, and highlight chips.
- **`#52525B` (Zinc-600 / Body Copy):** Primary reading layer providing 7.75:1 contrast against pure white, meeting WCAG AAA specifications.
- **`#71717A` (Zinc-500 / Meta & Captions):** Subservient micro-copy, timestamps, and metadata (4.83:1 contrast against white).

### Restricted Usages
- `zinc-400` (`#A1A1AA`) must never be applied to readable copy; its 2.56:1 contrast ratio breaches WCAG AA standards. It is confined strictly to disabled indicators and inactive graphical states.
- Ambient glow colors (`#7C3AED`, `#2563EB`, `#10B981`) remain restricted to radial canvas glows at an opacity range of 8% to 14%. They must never appear as solid backgrounds for text or controls.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings & Character Hierarchy
- **Bricolage Grotesque (Display & Headings):** High geometric presence and variable ink-trap character. Applied across `H1` through `H4` to provide editorial sophistication and tech studio credibility. Line heights are calibrated generously (e.g., a 1.43 ratio on `headline-md`) to accommodate wrapped portfolio and case-study headers without vertical cramping.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & User Interface):** Crisp, geometric, humanist sans-serif optimized for long-form scanability, metadata clarity, and interactive legibility across diverse screen densities.

### Paragraph Measure & Line-Length Rules
All sustained body copy blocks (including company overview paragraphs, lead texts, and service detail sections) enforce a strict maximum measure of `65ch` (`max-width: 65ch`). While parent section wrappers span wider grid containers, textual reading widths remain bounded to safeguard ocular return sweeps.

## Layout & Spacing

### 4px Modular Baseline Rhythm
The spacing engine operates entirely on an incremental 4px base: `4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`, and `128px`.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Viewport Framework
- **Mobile (375px Base):** Single-column service layouts, outer horizontal margins fixed at `16px` to `32px`. Critical hero messaging and dual CTAs must resolve entirely above the fold without horizontal scrolling.
- **Tablet (768px):** 2-column card structures across services and tech offerings; horizontal canvas padding at `48px`.
- **Desktop (1024px – 1440px):** 4-column structured services grid, 3-tier pricing layout. Global content width is capped at a centered maximum of `1200px` within outer bounds.

### Vertical Section Rhythms
- **Desktop Separation:** Standard spacing between distinct conceptual sections is `96px` (`section-gap-desktop`), alternating between `#FFFFFF` and `#FAFAFA` zebra bands.
- **Mobile Separation:** Standard vertical spacing drops to `64px` (`section-gap-mobile`).
- **Internal Card Padding:** Uniformly set to `24px` (`space-lg`) across service tiles, pricing packages, and portfolio items.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Surface Elevation
Card depth avoids heavy, muddy shadows and opaque borders. Surfaces leverage low-opacity ambient light models that let the underlying canvas breathe:
- **Resting Card Surface:** `0 4px 24px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)` creates a crisp lift that separates white surface cards from pure white or subtle zinc backgrounds without requiring an outline.
- **Interactive Hover Elevation:** `0 8px 32px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.10)` combined with a subtle `-2px` vertical translation (`translateY(-2px)`).

### Hairline Image Outlines
To prevent photography and client assets with light borders from bleeding into `#FFFFFF` card surfaces, all 16:9 media containers utilize a neutral transparent perimeter:
- **Token Rule:** `outline: 1px solid oklch(0 0 0 / 0.10)`. Colored or tinted borders (e.g., zinc or cool slate) are prohibited on photographic containers to keep visual focus on client content.

### Atmospheric Hero Gradient
Hero depth uses three layered radial gradients set directly behind primary headlines:
```css
background-image:
  radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 0%, rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12), transparent 50%),
  radial-gradient(ellipse at 15% 85%, rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.10), transparent 50%),
  radial-gradient(ellipse at 85% 85%, rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08), transparent 50%);
```

## Shapes

### Corner Radii Scale
The shape language combines crisp internal form controls with softened interactive triggers:
- **Small (`8px`):** Input fields, form select boxes, and micro utility tags.
- **Medium (`16px`):** Core component cards (Services, Tech Stack, Portfolio items).
- **Large (`24px`):** Featured hero containers, pricing tier highlights, and modal dialogues.
- **Pill (`9999px`):** All interactive buttons, status tags, badges, and the floating language switcher.

### Aspect Ratio Disciplines
- **Portfolio Media Containers:** Enforced strict `16:9` (`aspect-video`) container with `object-fit: cover`.
- **Avatars & Brand Icons:** Strict `1:1` square aspect ratio.
- **Client & Partner Vectors:** Normalized height (`32px` or `40px`) with `width: auto` to prevent visual distortion.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Zinc-900 (`#18181B`) background with pure white typography, fully pill-shaped (`9999px`). Hover state transitions smoothly over `150ms` to Zinc-800 (`#27272A`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Solid Emerald-700 (`#047857`) background with white typography for alternate actions, or subtle outlined pill using Hairline Border (`#E4E4E7`) with Zinc-900 text.
- **Target Sizes:** Touch devices enforce a minimum hit area of `44×44px`; desktop mouse environments enforce `40×40px`.
- **Accessible Focus Ring:** All interactive triggers use an explicit `focus-visible` ring of `2px solid #059669` (Emerald-600) with a `2px` offset.

### Chips & Badges
- **Informational Badges:** Tinted Emerald-50 (`#ECFDF5`) background paired with Emerald-800 (`#065F46`) text and a `9999px` border radius. Used for "Mulai dari" indicators and active service tiers.
- **Meta Chips:** Light Zinc-50 (`#FAFAFA`) surface with Zinc-600 (`#52525B`) text and a subtle 1px border (`#E4E4E7`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Resting State:** Pure white surface, 1px border using Zinc-300 (`#D4D4D8`), rounded to `8px`, horizontal padding of `16px`, height of `44px`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to Emerald-600 (`#059669`) with an offset focus glow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`).
- **Labels:** Rendered in Zinc-600 (`#52525B`), 14px regular font size positioned with 6px bottom gap.

### Cards
- **Architecture:** Unbordered white surfaces floating via `0 4px 24px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)` shadow. Corner radius fixed at `16px` (`24px` on pricing modules).
- **Featured / Highlighted Card:** Uses a subtle 1.5px border highlight using Emerald-500 (`#10B981`) or an inset border paired with the Emerald-50 surface header to denote recommended packages.

### Client Logo Infinite Marquee
- Continuous horizontal ticker powered by CSS keyframes (`translateX(-50%)`).
- Contains normalized single-color SVGs at `32px` to `40px` height.
- Pauses on `:hover` or when `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` is active.
